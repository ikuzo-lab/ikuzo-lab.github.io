研究紹介

Human Augmentation

We use XR technology to help people improve their physical skills and abilities. For example, the system can guide a beginner to learn a sport step by step. By making training easier and safer, we hope to support healthier daily habits.

Emotion Regulation

We explore how XR technology can help manage emotions. Our system tracks the user’s emotional state and provides feedback to help them stay calm or focused. This approach allows us to design more engaging experiences while also creating tools to prevent stress and support mental well-being.

Tools for Games Development

We develop tools related to game development such as new ways to interact with games, as well as plugins that make it easier for developers. These tools open up new possibilities of entertainment as well as supporting game developers community.
